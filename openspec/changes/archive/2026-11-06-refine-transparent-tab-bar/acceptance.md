# Conditional implementation acceptance

Verdict: accepted only when an authorized human directly merges the containing exact pull-request head after required current-head validation, explicitly asks the active agent to execute that protected exact-head merge while it is currently eligible, or personally enables native auto-merge for the unchanged head and GitHub integrates it after validation succeeds.

The maintainer integration decision accepts these scenarios:
- Transparent inactive tabs, overflow, and plus controls keep the terminal canvas at rest and use the same subtle background hover treatment as menus without recoloring labels or moving hit targets.
- Version and Time render and configure as one padded right-side pointer target, use a ` • ` separator, and retain valid constrained layouts; the Settings row and configuration dialog are labeled Widgets.
- On Black and Accent strips, `+` paints no separate black resting block; `+` and the grouped right side share a subtler lightened-canvas hover, with Black remaining neutral.
- The empty shell shows its editor, footer, and cwd; Enter on a non-empty draft creates one Agent, immediately echoes the prompt after the final design's leading breathing row with the full-width message treatment and configured timestamp, and submits only to that tab.
- The provisional submitted prompt remains pinned across pre-input and Working-only frames and hands off directly to the first coherent same-Agent surface containing the real submitted message, without a blank/editor flash.
- Creating or opening a tab that relocates `+` clears its old hover; the moved control remains at rest until a fresh pointer event reaches it.
- Accent inactive tabs inherit the accent canvas and its lighter hover; Black keeps its current dark inactive blocks and Transparent keeps text-only inactive tabs at rest.
