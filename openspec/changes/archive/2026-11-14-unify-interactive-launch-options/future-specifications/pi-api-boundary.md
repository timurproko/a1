## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public transports and programmatic entry reuse the existing guarded product services

Default Pi compatibility SHALL adapt documented public Pi APIs or owned implementations through the existing integration and composition owners. Interactive, print, JSON, RPC and the public A1 programmatic host/session entry SHALL share applicable session/model/settings/resource/tool services and A1 ownership guarantees. Public vendor types SHALL remain inside integration owners; product features SHALL consume neutral ports. Missing public facilities SHALL result in a concrete owned implementation or a tracked prerequisite, not private production imports, installed-file inspection, upstream CLI delegation or another agent/lifecycle service.

#### Scenario: Reuse public machine transports

- **WHEN** public print or RPC facilities are connected to A1's guarded runtime
- **THEN** their binding, stream, replacement and disposal hooks SHALL compose with existing product lifecycle ownership
- **AND** they SHALL not overwrite the shared API identity/readiness lifecycle or escape writer admission

#### Scenario: Publish programmatic access

- **WHEN** an external TypeScript host imports the declared A1 programmatic entry
- **THEN** its documented session configuration, events, replacement and secondary-session operations SHALL use the existing product service implementation with published types
- **AND** no private module path or raw upstream bypass SHALL be required to obtain applicable A1 guarantees

### Requirement: Compatibility evidence complements existing version and adoption authorities

The exact manifest/lockfile pin, existing public API baseline and existing feature-adoption inventory SHALL remain the compatibility authorities in their current owners. The default-compatibility engineering inventory SHALL link all documented public syntax, API, schema, event, settings and environment families to concrete behavior, owners, tests, differences, blockers and evidence levels. Historical interactive-only declined dispositions SHALL be reconciled rather than treated as permanent exclusions. A source/flag/method-presence check SHALL not certify unexercised public behavior.

Existing Pi-upgrade documentation and review expectations SHALL include reinspection of changed public contracts and affected actual SDK/packaged protocol tests before inheriting a compatibility claim for a new version. This SHALL NOT introduce another governance system, CI authority or runtime capability registry.

#### Scenario: Previously declined public contract

- **WHEN** a documented contract was historically declined only because A1 exposed interactive execution
- **THEN** the expanded default-compatibility plan SHALL assign its implementation/evidence outcome or specifically approved blocker/difference
- **AND** no old interactive-subset rationale SHALL count as completed work

#### Scenario: Candidate validation scope

- **WHEN** compatibility results are reported
- **THEN** they SHALL identify exact version/candidate and distinguish unit, actual SDK, packaged/development subprocess, platform and live external evidence
- **AND** incomplete platforms, private consumer assumptions or unexecuted live scenarios SHALL not be promoted to tested parity
